About Us

Nestled in the countryside between Edinburgh and Carlisle, Abbington Farm has grown from a small working farm into a popular visitor destination for locals and travellers alike. Today, visitors can enjoy our farm shop, stocked with fresh local produce and plants, relax in our welcoming café, meet friendly animals in the petting zoo, explore the play park, and take in the beauty of the surrounding countryside on our walking trails.

We are proud to offer a fun and memorable experience for visitors of all ages, from families looking for a great day out to garden enthusiasts, tourists, and those seeking a quality place to stop during their journey. Whether you're visiting for fresh food, outdoor activities, or simply a chance to unwind, you'll always receive a warm countryside welcome at Abbington Farm.
