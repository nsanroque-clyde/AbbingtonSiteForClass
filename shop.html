Abbington Farm Shop

At Abbington Farm Shop, we take pride in offering a wide range of fresh, locally produced food and drink. From free-range eggs, home-reared pork, and garden-fresh vegetables to homemade jams, preserves, honey, and handmade chocolates, there's something for everyone. We also stock freshly baked breads, cakes, award-winning pies, and a selection of locally sourced fruit juices, wines, beers, and ciders, bringing the very best of local produce to our customers.

Local Produce

Our free-range eggs are laid daily by the chickens on our farm.

We also offer home-reared pork from animals raised in the surrounding fields.

Fresh seasonal vegetables grown in our garden are available throughout the year.

Preserves

Our jams and preserves are made using fruit grown on the farm whenever possible.

We also stock local honey, marmalade and a range of traditional preserves.

For a special treat, browse our selection of handmade chocolates.

Bakery

Fresh bread is baked daily using quality ingredients.

Choose from a tempting selection of homemade cakes and sweet treats.

Our award-winning pies feature seasonal fillings, including apricot and blackberry.
