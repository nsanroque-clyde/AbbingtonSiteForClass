Farm Fun

There's plenty of fun for all ages at Abbington Farm Park. Visitors can enjoy a variety of seasonal attractions, including hayrides, pumpkin picking, and our popular corn maze. You can also meet the farm animals and learn about how they live, or get hands-on with traditional farm tools such as pitchforks and shovels. Whether you're exploring the farm or taking part in activities, there's always something new to discover.

Hayrides

Enjoy a relaxing tour of the farm on our traditional horse-drawn hayride.

Take in the countryside views and learn more about life on the farm.

Along the way, you'll have the chance to see our animals in their surroundings.

Corn Maze

Put your sense of direction to the test in our exciting corn maze.

Perfect for families and friends, it's a fun challenge for visitors of all ages.

Farmer for a Day

Step into the boots of a farmer and experience hands-on farm activities.

Try using traditional farm tools and learn how they are used around the farm.

Pick your own fresh fruit and vegetables to take home and enjoy.

Open Mondays to Saturdays 8am to 5pm, Sundays 9am to 430pm. Call us at 01864 502501.
