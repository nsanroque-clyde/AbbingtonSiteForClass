Abbington Farm Café

Welcome to Abbington Farm Café, where you can relax and enjoy delicious food made with fresh ingredients from our farm and local suppliers. From hearty breakfasts and light lunches to homemade cakes and hot drinks, our menu showcases the very best local produce, including fresh eggs, milk, yoghurt, cheese, and freshly baked bread. Whether you're stopping by after a walk or meeting friends for coffee, our café offers a warm welcome in a peaceful countryside setting.
