Petting Zoo & Play Park

There's plenty of fun for younger visitors at our Petting Zoo and Play Park. Meet our friendly farm animals, learn about how they are cared for, and enjoy hands-on experiences in a safe and welcoming environment. After visiting the animals, children can burn off some energy in our outdoor play area, packed with activities that encourage fun, exploration, and adventure.

Petting Zoo

Meet our friendly goats, sheep, and calves in a safe and welcoming environment.

Children can pet and help feed the animals under the supervision of our staff.

Play Park

Let the children enjoy our outdoor play area, complete with swings, slides, and climbing equipment.

It's the perfect place for young visitors to have fun and burn off some energy.

Park Walk

Take a stroll along our scenic pathways and explore the farm at your own pace.

Enjoy the fresh countryside air while discovering the animals, gardens, and attractions around the park.
